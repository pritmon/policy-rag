# Acme Corp — Employee Expense & Travel Policy
**Policy Number:** FIN-2024-007  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance & Compliance  
**Version:** 2.1

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes guidelines for business-related expenses and travel incurred by Acme Corp employees. It applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, and contractors acting on behalf of Acme Corp. Compliance is mandatory; violations may result in denial of reimbursement or disciplinary action.

---

## 2. General Principles

2.1 Employees must spend company funds responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.

2.2 All expenses must be pre-approved where required by this policy before being incurred.

2.3 Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of being incurred. Submissions after 30 days will be declined unless a documented exception is approved by the employee's VP.

2.4 Receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding **$25**. Expenses without required receipts will not be reimbursed.

2.5 Acme Corp does not reimburse personal expenses, fines, or any expense prohibited under this policy.

---

## 3. Travel Authorization

3.1 All domestic travel must be approved by the employee's direct manager **at least 3 business days** in advance via the Concur travel portal.

3.2 International travel requires approval from the employee's VP **at least 10 business days** in advance and must include a completed Travel Risk Assessment form (form HR-T-01).

3.3 Employees traveling internationally must register with the Corporate Security team and carry emergency contact cards provided by HR.

3.4 Travel for groups of five or more employees to the same destination must be approved by the CFO due to concentration-of-risk considerations.

---

## 4. Airfare

4.1 Domestic flights: economy class only. Business class is not permitted for domestic travel regardless of flight duration.

4.2 International flights exceeding **6 hours** (gate-to-gate): business class is permitted. Economy class must be booked for flights under 6 hours.

4.3 All flights must be booked through the Concur portal or an Acme-approved travel agent. Flights booked outside these channels will not be reimbursed.

4.4 The company's preferred carrier program should be used when fares are within **10% of the lowest available fare**.

4.5 Change and cancellation fees are reimbursable only if the change was required for business reasons and documented in Concur.

---

## 5. Hotels and Accommodation

5.1 Employees must use Acme-negotiated hotel rates where available. These are accessible through the Concur portal.

5.2 Maximum nightly rates (room rate excluding taxes and fees):
- **Tier 1 cities** (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): $350/night
- **Tier 2 cities** (Chicago, Boston, Seattle, Austin, Toronto): $250/night
- **All other locations**: $200/night

5.3 Stays exceeding the applicable cap require prior approval from the employee's VP and must document why lower-cost alternatives were unavailable.

5.4 Hotel loyalty points accrue to the employee personally; Acme does not purchase points or pay premiums to earn them.

5.5 Airbnb and other short-term rental platforms are not approved accommodation options unless all standard hotels in the area are fully booked, with written documentation of that fact.

---

## 6. Meals and Entertainment

6.1 **Per-diem meal allowances** (inclusive of tax and tip) when traveling overnight:
- Breakfast: $20
- Lunch: $30
- Dinner: $60
- **Daily total**: $110

6.2 Employees may expense actual meal costs up to the per-diem limit. Alcohol is **not reimbursable** as a meal expense under any circumstances.

6.3 **Business meals** (client or team entertainment) require a pre-approval from the employee's manager and must list all attendees and the business purpose. The limit is **$100 per person** per event.

6.4 Business entertainment exceeding $500 total requires VP approval and must be entered in Concur within 5 business days.

6.5 Meals with fewer than 2 external guests are not classified as "business entertainment" and fall under the standard per-diem.

---

## 7. Ground Transportation

7.1 Employees should use the most cost-effective ground transportation option. Economy rideshare (UberX, Lyft standard) is acceptable. Luxury tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are not reimbursable.

7.2 Rental cars are permitted when they are more cost-effective than rideshare for the duration of travel. Compact or mid-size vehicles only. Premium and SUV classes require manager approval.

7.3 Employees renting vehicles must use the corporate credit card to ensure Acme's CDW coverage applies. Personal card rentals are not covered by company insurance.

7.4 Parking fees at the destination are reimbursable. Airport long-term parking is reimbursable for trips of **3 days or less**. For trips over 3 days, employees should use off-site economy parking.

7.5 Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the **current IRS standard mileage rate** (updated annually; the Finance portal publishes the current year rate). Mileage claims must include a map printout or GPS log showing origin and destination.

---

## 8. Technology and Equipment

8.1 Employees may expense necessary data/roaming charges while traveling internationally (up to $50/day) without additional approval.

8.2 In-flight Wi-Fi is reimbursable up to $15 per flight for domestic and $30 per flight for international.

8.3 Equipment purchases (laptops, peripherals) are handled through IT procurement and are **not** submitted through the expense system.

---

## 9. Corporate Credit Card

9.1 All employees with a corporate credit card must use it for all business travel and entertainment expenses where accepted.

9.2 The corporate card is for business use only. Personal charges on the corporate card must be repaid within **5 business days** of the statement date or the employee will be subject to disciplinary action.

9.3 Lost or stolen corporate cards must be reported to the card provider and the Finance team within **2 hours** of discovery.

---

## 10. Submission and Approval Workflow

10.1 Expense reports are submitted in Concur. All required receipts must be uploaded as PDF or image attachments.

10.2 Reports under $500 are auto-approved pending receipt compliance and policy rules. Reports $500–$2,000 require manager approval. Reports over $2,000 require VP approval.

10.3 Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle (bi-weekly). Reports approved after the payroll cutoff are included in the following cycle.

10.4 Finance audits a random 10% sample of all expense reports for policy compliance. Employees found with systematic violations will be required to complete mandatory compliance training.

---

## 11. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly **not** reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Alcohol
- Personal entertainment (movies, sports events unrelated to client entertainment)
- Spouse or family member travel unless approved in advance as a documented business necessity
- Traffic or parking fines
- Gym or spa charges
- Clothing and personal grooming
- Gifts exceeding $25 per recipient per year (see Gifts Policy GFT-2024-001)
- Expenses submitted more than 30 days after being incurred

---

## 12. Policy Exceptions

12.1 Exceptions to any provision of this policy require written approval from the employee's VP and must be submitted to Finance before the expense is incurred (or within 24 hours for emergencies).

12.2 Systematic or repeated requests for exceptions will trigger a compliance review.

---

## 13. Policy Violations and Enforcement

13.1 Submitting a false expense claim is grounds for immediate termination and may result in legal action.

13.2 First-time unintentional violations will result in a coaching conversation and repayment of the non-compliant amount.

13.3 Repeated violations result in mandatory training, a formal written warning, and potential loss of corporate card privileges.
